# Further training of the diffusion model

## Project's description

Imagine that you want the diffusion model to be able to generate photos of you, your cat, or Cheburashka. 

However, the model does not initially understand what you, your cat, and Cheburashka look like. 

Therefore, you need to train the model to learn these concepts.

## Goal

1. Show that the diffusion model doesn't know what a Cheburashka is.
2. Fine-tune the LoRA on the diffusion model on a dataset of Cheburashka images.
3. Show that the diffusion model can generate a Cheburashka with the trained LoRA.
